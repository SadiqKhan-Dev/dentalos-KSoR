## Cleaning recall cadence

Most patients should visit for a cleaning and checkup every **6 months**.
Patients with active gum disease or other conditions may be placed on a
3-month or 4-month recall by their dentist. The recall agent checks the
patient's last visit date and schedules the next reminder accordingly.

## Appointment confirmation messages

Send an appointment confirmation **24 hours before** the scheduled time. The
message should include:

- Date and time of the appointment
- Clinic address (Smile Care Karachi, Plot 42, Block 7, Gulshan-e-Iqbal)
- A reminder to bring ID and insurance card
- A link or number to reschedule

## Reminder tone

- Be warm and professional — avoid clinical jargon.
- Use the patient's first name.
- Keep messages short (2–3 sentences for WhatsApp, 1 sentence for SMS).
- Never disclose treatment details in a reminder — keep it to logistics.
- If the patient has missed a previous appointment, acknowledge it gently
  without being accusatory.

## Escalation after missed appointments

If a patient misses two consecutive appointments without rescheduling, the
recall agent should:

1. Send a final courtesy message noting the missed visits.
2. Flag the patient record for staff review.
3. Stop automated reminders until a staff member manually re-enrolls the patient.
