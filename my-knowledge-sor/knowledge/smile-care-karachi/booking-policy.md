## Hours of operation

Smile Care Karachi is open Saturday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM PKT.
The clinic is closed on Fridays.

## Appointment vs. walk-in

We strongly recommend booking an appointment. Walk-ins are welcome but served
on a first-come, first-served basis — wait times vary and are longest on
Saturday mornings and weekday evenings.

Book through the DentalOS app, by calling +92-21-1234-5678, or via WhatsApp at
the same number.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

- **24-hour notice** is required for cancellation or rescheduling without a fee.
- Cancellations made fewer than 24 hours before the appointment may incur a
  **PKR 500 no-show fee**, deducted from any deposit or charged at the next visit.
- Patients who no-show twice in a rolling 3-month window will be asked to
  prepay for their next appointment.

## Deposits for specialist procedures

Procedures involving lab work (crowns, bridges, aligners) require a **50%
deposit** at the time of booking. The deposit is non-refundable once lab work
has commenced but may be applied to a rescheduled appointment if notice is
given 48 hours in advance.
