## Pricing

Consultation fees start at PKR 1,000. Procedure pricing varies — our team
provides a written estimate before any treatment begins. For procedures
requiring lab work, a detailed cost breakdown is shared during the consultation.

## Insurance

We are in-network with Adamjee Insurance, State Life Insurance, and EFU Health
Insurance. Please bring your insurance card to your first visit. For other
providers, we offer a receipt for reimbursement claims.

## First visit — what to bring

- Valid photo ID (CNIC or passport)
- Insurance card (if applicable)
- Any previous dental X-rays or records you have
- A list of current medications and allergies

## Payment methods

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. Installment plans are
available for treatment plans exceeding PKR 50,000 — ask at reception for
details.

## Emergency appointments

Dental emergencies (severe pain, swelling, trauma) are prioritized. Call our
helpline at +92-21-1234-5678 and press 1 for emergency triage. We aim to see
emergency patients within 2 hours during operating hours.
