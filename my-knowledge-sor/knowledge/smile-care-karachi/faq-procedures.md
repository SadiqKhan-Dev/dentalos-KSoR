## Root canal treatment

A root canal removes infected or damaged pulp from inside a tooth, cleans and
shapes the root canals, then fills and seals the tooth. The procedure is done
under local anaesthesia and typically takes 60–90 minutes per tooth. Most
patients return to normal activities the same day. A crown is usually placed on
the treated tooth afterward to restore its strength.

## Dental scaling and polishing

Scaling removes plaque and tartar buildup from above and below the gumline.
Polishing smooths the tooth surfaces to slow future buildup. The procedure
takes 30–45 minutes and is generally painless — some patients experience mild
sensitivity. We recommend scaling every 6 months for most patients.

## Teeth whitening

Professional whitening uses a peroxide-based gel applied to the teeth, either
in-office (results in about 1 hour) or with custom take-home trays (results
over 1–2 weeks). Sensitivity is common for 24–48 hours after treatment.
Whitening is not recommended for pregnant patients or children under 16.

## Dental crowns

A crown is a custom-made cap that covers a damaged tooth, restoring its shape,
size, and strength. The process involves two visits: the first to prepare the
tooth and take an impression, the second to fit the permanent crown. A
temporary crown is placed between visits. The entire process takes about 2 weeks.

## Fillings

Fillings repair cavities or minor tooth damage. The decayed material is
removed, the tooth is cleaned, and the cavity is filled with composite resin
(tooth-coloured) or amalgam (silver). The procedure takes 20–60 minutes
depending on the size and location of the cavity.

## Extractions

Extractions are performed under local anaesthesia. Simple extractions take
15–30 minutes. Surgical extractions (including wisdom teeth) may take longer
and require a follow-up visit. Post-extraction instructions and pain
management are provided at discharge.
