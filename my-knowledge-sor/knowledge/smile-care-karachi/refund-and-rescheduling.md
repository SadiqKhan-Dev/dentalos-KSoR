## Refund policy

- **Consultation fees** are non-refundable once the consultation has occurred.
- **Procedure deposits** (50% for lab-work procedures) are non-refundable once
  lab work has commenced. Before lab work begins, deposits may be refunded in
  full or applied to a rescheduled appointment with 48 hours' notice.
- **No-show fees** (PKR 500) are non-refundable.

## Exceptions requiring manager approval

The following require sign-off from the clinic manager (human:smile-care-admin):

- Refunds exceeding PKR 5,000.
- Refunds for procedures completed more than 7 days prior.
- Waiving a no-show fee for a patient with a documented medical emergency.
- Refunding a deposit after lab work has commenced due to a clinic error.

## Rescheduling exceptions

- Patients rescheduling within 24 hours due to a **medical emergency** (documented
  with a doctor's note or hospital record) are exempt from the no-show fee.
- Patients who reschedule more than 3 times for the same appointment should be
  flagged for a staff review conversation — not punitive, but to identify
  barriers to care.
- Specialist appointments (orthodontics, endodontics) rescheduled with less
  than 48 hours' notice may incur a **rebooking fee of PKR 1,000** to cover
  the specialist's reserved time.

## Recording refunds

All refunds must be recorded in the DentalOS billing module with:

- Patient ID
- Original payment method
- Refund amount and reason
- Approving staff member's name
- Date of refund
